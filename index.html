Privacy Policy — Quenly

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · App: com.briteenceladus.bptracker · Contact: stellarmatrixai@gmail.com

Personal wellness logging — not a medical device. Data you enter (blood pressure, heart rate, glucose, medications, notes) is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM). Quenly does not run a backend database of your health readings.

1. Data you enter (on-device)

  • Blood pressure and heart rate readings
  • Glucose readings
  • Medication list you enter
  • App settings and encryption material (salt + password verifier)

Your master password is never stored in plaintext. Biometric unlock is local convenience only. There is no password recovery.

2. Optional Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, identity such as email/name/sub may be stored on device. Health readings are not uploaded to Google or Quenly servers. Sign-in does not replace the master password.

3. Optional Quenly Pro (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The app keeps a local premium flag. Health readings are not sent as part of billing.

4. Expo Updates (OTA)

The app may contact Expo to download app updates. That is not a health-data upload.

5. AI insights

Optional AI insights are disabled in this build. They are not active today.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs
  • No analytics SDKs for product analytics
  • No Quenly server database of health readings
  • No sale of personal health data

7. Exports you initiate

CSV, PDF, and encrypted backups leave the app only when you start them.

8. Deletion

Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall the app.

9. Children

Quenly is not directed at children under 13.

Not a medical device. Quenly does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Talk to a clinician about your health.
